# Surface brief: e-Licensing landing and app shell

Scope: whole-app redesign, first surface /home. Visitor mode: Persuade on /home; Operate on the rest, same world, quieter.
Audience: Thai merchants and SMEs. Job: start a license application, then track it. Action: เริ่มลงทะเบียนคำขอ (/home/merchant).
Constraints: MUI + SCSS stay. Thai first. No invented agencies, numbers, or affiliation; stamp examples are labeled as samples.
Unresolved: hero copy still names hotels only while the product covers 13 merchant types (flag to user).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The app is a paper application form that agencies stamp. The category-default refused: blue portal with hero photo and a grid of service cards.
OWN-WORLD: Desk-grey ground, near-white form sheets with 1px graphite ruled boxes and square corners, ballpoint-blue ink for filled values and primary action, rubber-stamp red for serials and corrections, Sarabun for printed form text, Mitr for form headings and stamp lettering, stamps rotated slightly with a double border.
STORY: A merchant sees one form, understands filing once leads to stamps from several agencies, and starts the application.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: one tall form sheet fills the left two thirds under a thin ruled nav of boxed cells; the form heading band and Thai title sit top, the primary blue button and a secondary outline button below, then the four steps as ruled rows. Right third: sample stamp slots for three agencies, labeled as sample, with one stamp thunking in on load.
FORM: Rubber-Stamp Form, from the safer re-roll hand, position 2 of the grounded list; seed: none (unavailable).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
